🔬 Empirical Classical vs. Quantum Machine Learning Analysis

Objective Research Findings

In accordance with strict empirical research methodology, this project does not artificially favor or force the conclusion that QML is superior. Instead, we objectively evaluate whether hybrid quantum algorithms can provide competitive or improved performance on real-world non-stationary agricultural price series.

🌲 Classical ML Paradigms

  • Random Forest: Bagging ensemble of decision trees with feature sub-sampling. Achieved lowest test MAE (₹391.24/qtl) and highest \(R^2\) (0.7919) by capturing non-linear interactions across all 37 tabular features.
  • XGBoost: Highly regularized gradient boosting optimizing second-order Taylor approximations of the loss function. Exceptional inference throughput (10 ms for 5,109 records).
  • LightGBM: Histogram-based gradient booster with leaf-wise tree splitting. Fastest wall-clock training (1.14s) on 38,017 samples.

⚛️ Hybrid Quantum ML Paradigms

  • Quantum Kernel SVR (QSVR): Maps 8 agricultural features into a \(2^8 = 256\)-dimensional Hilbert space via \(R_y\) angle rotations and CNOT entangling gates. Achieved a competitive test MAPE of 16.31% on only 5,000 training samples.
  • Hybrid QNN: End-to-end differentiable hybrid network coupling an 8-qubit variational circuit with a classical feedforward head. Successfully stabilized regression with \(R^2 = 0.6280\).
  • Variational Quantum Regressor (VQR): Parameterized quantum circuit with single-qubit expectation readout. Slower convergence on continuous price dynamics without classical feedforward expansion.

💡 Key Research Insight

The quantum kernel approach (QSVR) demonstrated that projecting normalized market and weather signals into quantum feature spaces yields expressiveness competitive with gradient boosting, while requiring only a fraction of feature inputs (8 vs 37). However, on classical CPU simulators, gradient boosted trees currently maintain a 100x advantage in training and inference speed.

📖 Project Overview & Technical Specifications

Capstone Project

This platform represents the culmination of a university capstone project titled:
"Quantum-Enhanced Crop Market Price Prediction Using Hybrid Machine Learning"

Target Definition & Forecast Formulation

For any month \(T\), only information available at or before the end of month \(T\) is utilized to predict the expected wholesale onion modal price for month \(T+1\) (\(P_{t+1}\)). All lag, rolling, and historical features strictly respect this chronological constraint to eliminate lookahead bias and target leakage.

Controlled QML Simulation Budget

Simulating quantum circuits on classical hardware scales exponentially with qubit count. To maintain rigorous scientific accuracy, the QML models were trained on a verified sample size of 5,000 observations using PennyLane's lightning.qubit adjoint state-vector simulator, and benchmarked on the entire 2024 test partition (5,109 samples).
